Our Journey & Legacy

Transforming Healthcare Access from Prescription to Doorstep

MedCare was founded in 2018 with a singular mission: to guarantee that every patient, regardless of location, receives 100% authentic, unadulterated medication at fair and transparent prices.

Recognizing that counterfeit pharmaceuticals and erratic pricing plague conventional brick-and-mortar chemist networks, our team of clinical doctors and pharmacologists established a direct-from-manufacturer supply chain. Every single tablet, injection, and wellness supplement is procured directly from WHO-GMP certified pharmaceutical leaders such as Cipla, Sun Pharma, and Abbott.

  • Double Pharmacist Verification: Every prescription is scrutinized by a licensed clinician to verify dosages, contraindications, and course durations.
  • Zero Counterfeit Guarantee: Direct sourcing with tamper-evident batch authentication codes on every dispatched pack.
  • State-of-the-Art Cold Chain: Temperature-controlled storage ensuring biologicals, insulin, and sensitive vaccines preserve maximum therapeutic potency.

Our Purpose & Aspiration

Guided by clinical ethics, compassionate care, and digital innovation, we work around the clock to improve patient outcomes nationwide.

Our Mission

To provide safe, reliable, and affordable pharmaceuticals to every patient across India by combining certified clinical expertise with modern digital fulfillment, making healthcare painless, prompt, and dignified.

  • Eliminate counterfeit drugs through end-to-end verified sourcing.
  • Deliver essential and chronic medicines within hours of prescription upload.
  • Provide free, 24/7 pharmacist dosage advisory to patients in need.

Our Vision

To establish MedCare as South Asia’s most trusted healthcare ecosystem, pioneering AI-driven prescription safety, personalized medicine management, and zero-compromise cold-chain logistics.

  • Expand automated cold-chain logistics to tier-2, 3, and rural communities.
  • Integrate proactive chronic disease management and refill adherence automation.
  • Drive down patient healthcare costs by up to 25% through direct procurement.
