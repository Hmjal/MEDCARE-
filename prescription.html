Safety & Compliance

Prescription Guidelines

To avoid delivery delays, ensure your uploaded prescription contains the key legal elements shown below.

CITY HEALTHCARE CLINIC Dr. Rajesh V. Sharma, M.D., M.B.B.S. Reg. No: KMC-49281-2012
OFFICIAL RX
Patient: Rahul Verma, 42 Y / M Date: 18-Oct-2026
℞
1. Tab. Dolo 650mg 1 Tab TID (After meals) x 5 days
2. Cap. Augmentin 625mg 1 Cap BD (Morning & Night) x 7 days
3. Tab. Pantocid 40mg 1 Tab OD (Before breakfast) x 7 days
Dr. R. Sharma Doctor's Signature & Seal

What Makes a Valid Prescription:

  • Doctor & Hospital Details:

    Doctor's full name, medical qualification, clinic header, and registered medical council number.

  • Patient Details & Date:

    Patient's name, age/gender, and consultation date (must not be older than 6 months for acute medications).

  • Clear Medicine Names & Dosages:

    Legible drug brand or active generic name with strength (e.g. 500mg, 10ml) and dosage frequency.

  • Doctor's Signature & Stamp:

    Physical handwritten signature or authenticated digital e-signature.

Note: We cannot accept cropped images that hide the doctor's header, blurred photos, or prescriptions with handwritten alterations.
